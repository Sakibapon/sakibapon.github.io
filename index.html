Welcome

I am a PhD Student at the University of Vermont, focusing on Agentic AI for home hemodialysis monitoring and LLM-based clinical decision support. My dissertation is titled Agentic AI Frameworks for Clinical Decision Support in Home Hemodialysis. Previously, I completed my MSc in Computer Science at the University of Maine and my BSc at BRAC University.

My research bridges explainable AI (XAI) in medical imaging and multi-agent LLM systems for clinical decision support. I have published 11+ peer-reviewed papers and have experience in LLM fine-tuning, RAG pipelines, GRPO, and inference optimization.

Research Interests

  • Large Language Models (LLMs): Fine-tuning, RAG, RLHF, LoRA, QLoRA, PEFT, vLLM, Distributed Training
  • Agentic AI & Multi-Agent Systems: Clinical decision support, autonomous agents, RLHF
  • Explainable AI (XAI): Medical image analysis, model interpretability, clinical decision support
  • Computer Vision: Medical imaging, object detection, action recognition, inference optimization
  • Distributed & Edge AI: Resource-efficient deployment, inference optimization, Weights & Biases
